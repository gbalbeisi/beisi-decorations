Questions

Before you reserve.

Will it hold up outside all winter?

Yes. Every box is sealed, primed and painted with exterior-grade paint, and the edges are sealed so they don't soak up moisture. It is built to stand on a lawn from November through January.

How fast can I get it?

Estimated delivery is 1 to 2 days after we confirm your order. During the busiest weeks of December, reserve early to lock in your date.

How much is delivery?

Delivery and setup is $90 to $120, depending on your address. We confirm the exact amount when we confirm your order. Pickup in Canton is included in the $899 price.

Where do I keep it after the holidays?

The box bolts together, so it comes apart into flat panels in a few minutes. The panels stand against a garage or basement wall until next season.

What if I'm ready to replace it someday?

Our sister company, Beisi Removal, can haul it away for you when you're done with it.
